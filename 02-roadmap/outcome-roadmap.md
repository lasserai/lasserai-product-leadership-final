## 2. Trade-off memo

> **Rock #1:** #4 — Simplify the daily log from 14 required fields to 4. Directly addresses foreman feedback and reduces friction in daily field reporting.
>
> **Rock #2:** #1 — Build an offline-first mobile experience.Field teams often work with poor connectivity; reliable offline use removes a major adoption barrier.
>
> **Rock #3:** #7 — Create a foreman-facing mobile app.Gives field teams an experience designed for their actual workflow rather than adapting the enterprise PM interface.
>
> **Hard No #1:** #10 — Build an executive reporting dashboard with custom KPIs.Commercial pressure from at-risk renewals is real, but this does not directly solve field adoption.
>
> **Hard No #2:** #12 — Add a time-tracking module.Contract negotiations may create urgency, but a new module expands scope without addressing the core field adoption problem.
>
> **Hard No #3:** #13 — Refresh the web UI.Cosmetic improvements based on prospect feedback are less important than fixing the field experience and core usability barriers.
