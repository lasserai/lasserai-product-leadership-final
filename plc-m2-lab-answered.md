## Prioritize your Rocks and your Hard Nos

### Rock #1: Field-first, offline-capable capture experience
Make Meridian the easiest and most reliable way for foremen and field users to capture actionable observations where the work happens, including when connectivity is poor.

**Strategic rationale:** If connectivity prevents capture at the moment an observation occurs, the core observation → action → resolution → learning loop breaks. This supports end-to-end workflow adoption and repeat behavior.

**Related OKRs:** KR1 — Workflow adoption; KR2 — Repeat behavior / habit.

### Rock #2: Radically simplify field reporting from 14 required fields to 4
Reduce the administrative effort required to report an issue so field users can capture it once and return to the work.

**Strategic rationale:** Fourteen mandatory fields create friction and conflict with the strategy of resolving important observations without turning field users into administrators. Test the hypothesis that less friction increases repeat behavior and habit. Validate that the remaining fields still meet compliance and downstream needs.

**Related OKRs:** KR2 — Repeat behavior / habit; KR4 — Efficiency.

### Rock #3: Close the loop with actionable notifications
Make ownership and follow-through immediate and visible so captured observations trigger accountable action rather than becoming another record to manage.

**Strategic rationale:** Notifications can strengthen routing and closed-loop action once the core capture workflow works. Define which person needs to act and what event triggers the notification; the current schedule-change notification concept does not automatically prove faster observation-to-action time.

**Related OKR:** KR3 — Reduce median time from observation to accountable action.

### ❌ Hard No #1: Time-tracking module
Do not expand Meridian into a broad construction software suite with an adjacent time-tracking category that has little connection to the winning aspiration.

**Why:** Customer demand alone does not make a capability strategically important. Time tracking does not strengthen the observation-to-resolution loop Meridian has chosen to win.

### ❌ Hard No #2: Custom executive KPI dashboard
Do not build a bespoke dashboard product that pulls Meridian away from field execution and toward business intelligence.

**Boundary:** Make operational data easy to consume in existing enterprise reporting instead. However, because two renewals were described as being at risk, revisit this decision if the revenue exposure is material, the dashboard is genuinely decisive for renewal, and no workable alternative exists. A narrowly scoped dashboard could then protect the strategy rather than distract from it.

### ❌ Hard No #3: Cosmetic web UI refresh
Do not prioritize a cosmetic refresh based only on feedback that the interface looks outdated.

**Why:** There is no demonstrated connection to the Number One or the current OKRs. Reconsider only if evidence shows that the interface prevents users from completing the core workflow.

## Roadmap summary: Now / Next / Later

### 🟢 NOW: Prove the core behavior

1. Make Meridian the easiest and most reliable way to capture an actionable field observation where the work happens. → **OKR 1: End-to-end adoption**
2. Make field users choose Meridian again by materially reducing the effort required to report and follow an issue. → **OKR 2: Habit + efficiency**
3. Make captured observations reliably trigger accountable action instead of becoming another record to manage. → **OKR 3: Faster action**

These bets align with the Rocks: field-first/offline capture, reducing the daily log from 14 to 4 fields, and actionable notifications.

### 🔵 NEXT: Strengthen the complete loop

1. Make resolution as effortless and visible as capture. First prove that users capture observations and initiate action consistently, then optimize the rest of the observation → resolution journey.
2. Make structured context emerge from field work without creating additional administration. Once the core workflow is habitual, enrich observations with context needed for downstream use.
3. Extend the loop across additional participants, such as subcontractors, after proving the workflow with the primary superintendent/foreman user.
4. Make Meridian operational data easy to consume in enterprise reporting. Enterprise visibility becomes more valuable once field activity creates useful structured data.
5. Use completed observation → action → outcome loops to improve the next user experience. Learning should be a flywheel built from completed workflows, not an AI objective in itself.

### ⚪ LATER: Strategic bets, not commitments

- **AI-assisted workflow support:** Help users turn accumulated context into better next actions once sufficient real workflow evidence exists.
- **Multi-project scaling:** Expand after the core behavior has been proven within individual project workflows.
- **Automated compliance support:** Invest where it demonstrably strengthens the observation-to-resolution loop.
- **Richer field context:** Add photo annotation and relevant contextual information when evidence shows it improves action or resolution.
- **Broader ecosystem participation:** Add other actors where they strengthen rather than dilute the core workflow.

### Roadmap storyline

**NOW:** Prove the habit → **NEXT:** Strengthen the end-to-end loop and reuse its data → **LATER:** Scale and intelligently augment the proven loop.

First win the field behavior, then make better use of the trusted workflow, then scale and augment it.

## Strategy and measurement reference

**Strategic constraint:** No investment that does not strengthen the observation → action → resolution → learning loop.

**Number One:** Percentage of actionable field observations managed end-to-end through Meridian — not logins, uploads, or feature usage.

**Objective:** Make Meridian the habitual path from significant field observation to accountable action.

- **KR1 — Workflow adoption:** Increase the share of qualifying observations managed end-to-end through Meridian from X% to Y%.
- **KR2 — Repeat behavior:** Increase the proportion of users who return to Meridian for their next qualifying field workflow from X% to Y%.
- **KR3 — Value:** Reduce median time from observation to accountable action from X to Y.
- **KR4 — Efficiency:** Reduce manual and duplicate field reporting effort by X%.

The targets are intentionally left as placeholders because the source documents do not provide baseline values or final numerical targets.
