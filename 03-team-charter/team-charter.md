## Lab notes

**Name the situation**

- **Who they are (role, not name), what you have observed, and how long it has been happening.:** A colleague on my product team has been missing agreed deadlines and providing status updates later than expected for the past two months. In team meetings, they often raise concerns about tasks after work has started, and other team members have had to adjust their priorities to accommodate the delays. I have also observed that they contribute actively during discussions but do not always follow through on the actions they agree to complete.

**Make a diagnosis**

- **Your diagnosis, plus one sentence on why. Is your frustration with their behavior, or with a decision you made?:** Expectations and deadlines have been agreed upon, but missed deadlines, late status updates, and incomplete follow-through have continued for two months without being addressed directly. My frustration is primarily with the repeated behavior, but I also recognize that I am responsible for not having raised the pattern clearly and early enough.

**Write your opening line**

- **One next action I will take in the next two weeks is:** Schedule a one-to-one conversation with my colleague this week to discuss the repeated missed deadlines and late status updates, agree on clear expectations for progress reporting, and establish a follow-up date to review improvement.
- **The first sentence of the conversation I need to have is:** “In our last two project sprints, when we agreed on deadlines, you missed several of them and provided status updates later than expected, which meant other team members had to adjust their priorities and our work was delayed.”

**AI role-play**

- **After you step out: what did the role-play change about how you will open this conversation for real?:** I will open the conversation by describing the missed deadlines and their impact on the team, while also inviting my colleague to share their perspective. Instead of making them feel blamed, I will ask what is preventing realistic deadlines and how we can improve workload planning and communication together. I will still address the repeated behavior directly, but focus on understanding the causes, agreeing on clear expectations, and establishing concrete actions to prevent future delays.

**Show and swap your team charter**

- **Where does the charter leave room for interpretation that could cause a conflict?:** The charter does not clearly define qualifies as an urgent enterprise commitment or when an exception to the field adoption strategy is justified. This could create conflicts between Sales and Product when priorities change.
